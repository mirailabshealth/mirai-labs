Research literacy

Understand the evidence before the claim.

Human trials

Look for a comparison group, the population studied, the endpoint and the duration. A change in a hormone or biomarker is not necessarily a meaningful health benefit.

Cells & animals

These studies help explain mechanisms and generate hypotheses. Human cells grown in a laboratory are not a clinical trial, and animal results may not translate to people.

Combinations & blends

Separate studies of two ingredients do not prove their combination works. Direct combination evidence, duplicated ingredients and shared pathways must be evaluated separately.
