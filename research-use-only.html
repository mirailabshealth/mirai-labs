Research Use Only Policy

Version RUO-2026-10-03 · Mirai Labs

Our materials are supplied solely for legitimate laboratory research and analytical work by qualified purchasers. This policy applies to research materials ordered through Mirai Labs and to representations made about those materials.

For laboratory research only. Not for human or animal consumption or administration. Not for medical, diagnostic, therapeutic, cosmetic or veterinary use.
Purchaser eligibilityProhibited usesLaboratory responsibilitiesOrder review

1. Qualified purchasers

Purchasers must be at least 21 years old, authorized to purchase for their laboratory or research organization, and able to handle the materials safely in an appropriate laboratory environment. Purchasers must have the training, facilities and procedures needed for their intended lawful research.

Provide accurate contact, organization and shipping details. Creating an account or submitting a request does not establish eligibility or guarantee acceptance. Mirai Labs may request information about the organization, intended research and handling arrangements before approving an order.

2. Uses we do not permit

Mirai Labs research materials must not be:

  • Injected, ingested, inhaled, applied to the body, or otherwise administered to any person or animal.
  • Used for self-experimentation, personal wellness, weight management, athletic performance, anti-aging or other personal use.
  • Used to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent disease, or used in clinical care, diagnostic testing of patients or veterinary care.
  • Prepared, compounded, repackaged or incorporated into medicines, supplements, foods, cosmetics or other products intended for human or animal use.
  • Resold or transferred for a prohibited purpose, or represented as suitable or approved for such use.

A research-use label does not authorize an otherwise unlawful activity. Purchasers remain responsible for ensuring that their proposed work meets applicable requirements.

3. Scientific information and product documentation

Product descriptions, scientific references and educational content describe research subjects; they are not medical advice, dosing instructions or recommendations for personal use. Mirai Labs does not provide human or animal administration protocols or advice on selecting materials for health outcomes.

Listed milligram amounts describe material quantity, not a dose. A purity result or certificate of analysis describes the tested sample and reported methods; it does not establish clinical safety, sterility, suitability for injection or regulatory approval. Review the documentation for the specific lot and request clarification before research use if information is missing or unclear.

4. Laboratory responsibilities

Before handling a material, assess its hazards and review available product documentation, storage instructions and safety information. Use appropriate laboratory procedures, trained personnel, protective equipment and access controls.

Keep materials labeled and secured away from children, pets, food and personal-use products. Follow suitable storage, transport, spill-response and waste-disposal procedures, along with applicable laws and institutional requirements. Do not assume that an incompletely characterized material is safe.

If labeling, packaging or documentation appears incorrect, or a product arrives damaged, set it aside and contact Mirai Labs with the order and lot reference before using it. See our order-issue policy.

5. Order review and enforcement

Orders require Mirai Labs approval before payment instructions are issued. We may decline or cancel an order, request additional verification, or restrict account access when information is incomplete or there is reason to believe materials will be used contrary to this policy.

Order approval is not regulatory authorization or verification that a proposed experiment is lawful or safe. If a paid order cannot be fulfilled, the unfulfilled portion is handled under our payment and refund policies and applicable law.

6. Affiliates and representations

Affiliates and anyone representing Mirai Labs must describe our materials consistently with this policy. They must not promote personal use, make medical or health-outcome claims, provide administration instructions, or imply that research materials are approved medicines. Approval as an affiliate does not authorize such claims.

7. Acknowledgment and updates

Customers must acknowledge this policy during registration, account setup and each new order request. Future revisions apply prospectively; the version presented with an accepted order governs that order. Nothing in this policy removes rights or obligations that cannot be excluded under applicable law.

Questions about eligibility, documentation or a suspected misuse? Contact mirai.labs.health@gmail.com. Include an order reference if available; do not send payment credentials or sensitive personal records.
