Before you get started

Where will I complete my assessment?

Program links open Agile Telehealth. The assessment takes place through the partner’s OpenLoop intake. Please enter personal health information only in the clinical service’s intake or patient portal, not in a Mirai education inquiry.

Where do existing patients sign in?

Use patient login ↗ to access the Agile Telehealth patient portal. This is separate from any historical Mirai Labs account.

Are research-library compounds offered as treatments?

No such assumption should be made. Our learning library discusses scientific research, including experimental compounds. Inclusion in that library does not mean a compound is offered by the partner or suitable for human use.
