Payments after approval

Submit your request first. Mirai Labs reviews availability and confirms the total, including shipping and applicable tax, before requesting payment. An unpaid request is not a completed purchase.

Manual payment arrangements may include Zelle, Venmo, or Cash App. The available method and verified recipient details will be provided directly for your approved order. Do not send funds before receiving those instructions. Credit cards and Apple Pay are not currently enabled.

Use your order reference with your payment. Mirai Labs checks receipt in its payment account before marking an order paid or shipping it. A screenshot or customer payment notification alone is not confirmation. Keep your receipt and check your account portal for order status.

Shipping & delivery

We review requests for delivery within the United States, including Puerto Rico. Standard shipping is $15 per order, or free when the product subtotal is $250 or more after all quantity and partner discounts, before tax. Shipping does not count toward the $250 threshold. Standard shipments use protective packaging; carrier service and dispatch timing are confirmed before payment. Same-day dispatch and delivery dates are not guaranteed. For Puerto Rico addresses, select or enter PR as the state/territory and include the urbanization when applicable.

Orders move to fulfillment after approval and verification of payment. Carrier transit estimates begin after carrier acceptance and are not guaranteed arrival dates. Tracking is added to your account portal after dispatch; a created label alone does not mean the carrier has received the package.

Check your delivery address carefully. Contact us immediately if it needs correction; changes may not be possible after dispatch. If an address error causes additional delivery costs, we will discuss them with you before arranging another shipment.

If we cannot ship within the agreed timeframe, we will notify you with an updated estimate and offer cancellation and a prompt refund where required. If we cannot fulfill a paid order, we will refund the unfulfilled portion and associated charges as applicable.

For missing, delayed or damaged shipments, contact us with your order reference and tracking number. We will review the carrier information and help resolve the issue. See the order-issue process below.

All sales are final

All sales are final once payment is verified and the order is accepted for fulfillment, subject to the exceptions below and applicable law. We do not offer change-of-mind returns, exchanges or cancellations for research materials. An unpaid request may be withdrawn before fulfillment.

Research materials cannot be accepted back into saleable inventory because storage and handling outside our control cannot be verified. Do not return any product without written instructions from Mirai Labs.

Incorrect, damaged or missing orders

We review incorrect items, delivery damage, confirmed shipment loss and documented product-quality concerns. Contact mirai.labs.health@gmail.com promptly, preferably within 48 hours of delivery for visible damage or an incorrect item. This reporting request does not remove rights provided by law or exclude a problem that could not reasonably be discovered within that period.

Include your order reference, a description of the issue, and relevant photos of the product, packaging and shipping label. For a quality concern, include the batch or lot number. Retain the materials and packaging while we review the case; do not send sensitive financial information.

Depending on the verified issue, resolution may include replacement, reshipment or refund. Store credit is offered only with your agreement. Nothing in this policy limits a refund or other remedy required by applicable law. Any approved refund will be coordinated through the original payment method where possible.

Policy changes

The policy presented when your order is accepted applies to that order. Future revisions apply prospectively.
