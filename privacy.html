Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026 · Version PRIVACY-2026-10-12

Scope and contact

This policy covers visitors, educational correspondents and people whose previous account or guest-application records remain with Mirai Labs. An account is not required to make a privacy request. Contact mirai.labs.health@gmail.com.

Current educational inquiries

The follow-up form prepares an email containing the name, email address, selected compounds and notes you enter. It does not transmit those fields automatically. Your email provider processes the message when you send it; Mirai Labs receives it through Google/Gmail and uses it to answer your inquiry. Do not include sensitive health information, passwords, financial credentials or identity documents. An inquiry is not consent to unrelated marketing.

Browser storage and hosting

Age acknowledgment and research selections are stored in your browser. You can clear them through browser settings. This educational release does not load the optional analytics collector or advertising pixels. Previously recorded analytics are not erased by this change; existing raw-event retention settings remain in the service. GitHub Pages hosts the site. Some pages load Google Fonts and Tailwind resources. Hosting and resource providers receive connection information, such as IP address, browser details and access times, needed to operate their services.

Historical records and providers

The earlier account and application systems are closed to new public submissions. Supabase continues to hold protected historical account, financial, support and application records. These can include names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, organization details, correspondence, reference numbers and policy acknowledgments. Former guest applications additionally included platform, social handle or profile link, application statement, consent version, submission reference, review status and timestamps. Submission did not require an account.

The former public application used Supabase directly, not Formspree. Earlier Formspree submissions may remain with that provider or in correspondence records. Google/Gmail handles communications. Authorized owners use historical records for support, security, resolving disputes and applicable recordkeeping purposes. We do not provide contact lists to former partners for their marketing. Disclosure may occur when legally required or necessary to investigate abuse and protect rights.

Retention and safeguards

Retention depends on the record and purpose, including legal and security requirements; no new fixed retention period is introduced here. Existing access controls continue to protect records. Former guest applications are not publicly readable. A website revision does not delete records or backups automatically. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

Request access, correction, account closure or deletion by emailing us from the address associated with your record. Former guest applicants may also request withdrawal from consideration. We may need to verify identity and clarify the request. Some records may need to remain under applicable legal or security requirements. Do not send identity documents unless an appropriate verification process has been arranged. You can ask to stop optional marketing communications without affecting necessary service communications. Providing a phone number does not consent to marketing texts.

Age, external services and updates

This resource is intended for adults aged 21 and over. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information. External services have their own privacy practices and may process information in multiple jurisdictions. Material changes and any necessary additional consent will be handled as required by applicable law. This revision does not authorize an undisclosed incompatible use of previously collected information.
