Privacy Policy

Version PRIVACY-2026-10-08

1. Scope

This policy describes information handled through the Mirai Labs website, account portal, research inquiries, affiliate applications and order support. It applies to visitors and affiliate applicants who have not created accounts, as well as account holders, and to our current operation, including manual payments after owner approval. For privacy questions, contact mirai.labs.health@gmail.com.

2. Information you provide

Account and order information includes your name, email address, phone number, laboratory or organization, shipping recipient and address, selected materials, quantities, order history, correspondence and policy acknowledgments. Supabase processes your email and password for authentication. Public affiliate applications collect your full name, email address, chosen platform, social handle or profile/website link, and statement about your audience and interest in partnering. We also store the application consent version, submission time, a unique submission reference used to avoid duplicate processing, review status and review time. Applying does not create an account. If you later activate an approved application, we link it to your verified account using the matching email address. Activated affiliate records also include referral codes and commission records. Payment records include the method, amount, transaction reference and verification status. Do not send passwords, full card details, bank-login credentials, identity documents or medical records in application statements, order notes or ordinary email.

3. Technical information and browser storage

Hosting, authentication and other service providers may process IP addresses, browser or device information, access times and security logs when serving the site. The site uses browser storage for age acknowledgment, saved research selections and authentication sessions. Clearing storage may sign you out or remove saved selections. Optional first-party analytics runs only after you select Allow analytics. It records a random session identifier, event time, a page label, and a compound identifier for product views, catalog selections and cart actions, plus a request-submission event. It does not include your name, email, phone, address, order ID, payment information, full URL, URL parameters or referral source. Session identifiers are stored in session storage and rotate after 30 minutes of inactivity. The analytics choice is saved in local storage. We respect browser Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals by disabling optional analytics. You can change your choice using Analytics preferences at the bottom of each page. Opting out stops future events but does not undo aggregated counts already recorded. The site does not use advertising pixels. Third-party resources such as fonts and software libraries are loaded from external providers, which receive connection information needed to deliver those resources.

4. How information is used

We use information to create and secure accounts, verify email, assess requests, communicate approved totals and payment instructions, verify manual payments, arrange shipping, answer support questions, investigate fraud or order issues, maintain business records and administer approved affiliate referrals. We use guest application information to assess partnership suitability, review the profile or website you provide, contact you about your application and decision, prevent duplicate or abusive submissions, and link an approved application to a verified account if you choose to activate it. Agreement to application-related contact is not consent to unrelated marketing. A partner code is associated with an order to calculate the applicable discount and commission. Providing a phone number does not opt you into marketing texts.

5. Service providers and sharing

The site uses GitHub Pages for hosting, Supabase for authentication and storage of account, order and affiliate application data, and Google/Gmail for email communications, including application-related messages and owner notifications. The public affiliate form sends submissions directly from your browser to our Supabase-backed application service for storage and owner review; it does not submit them to Formspree. Formspree was used for earlier inquiry and affiliate forms; records previously submitted through those forms may remain in the associated service or correspondence records. Some pages load Google Fonts, Tailwind and jsDelivr resources. Payment providers receive information directly when you pay through their services; carriers receive the contact and address details needed to deliver an order. These providers process information under their applicable terms and privacy notices. Authorized Mirai Labs owners access information needed to operate the business. Affiliate reporting provides referral and commission information; it is not intended to disclose customer shipping addresses, phone numbers or payment credentials. We may disclose information when legally required or necessary to investigate fraud and protect rights. We do not provide customer contact lists to affiliates for their marketing.

6. Retention and safeguards

We retain account, order, payment, affiliate (including guest applications and their review records) and correspondence records as needed to operate the service, resolve disputes and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. Optional raw analytics events are automatically deleted after 90 days. Owner reports show aggregate browsing activity and business metrics; browsing identifiers are not linked to customer accounts. Other retention depends on the record and purpose; closing an account does not necessarily erase completed transaction records or backups immediately. Access controls restrict account, order and affiliate application access by role. Guest applications are not publicly readable through the application service; authorized owners can review them. Submission does not give an applicant access to other applications. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Use a unique password, sign out on shared devices and report suspected unauthorized access promptly.

7. Your choices and requests

You can update contact and shipping details in the account portal. To request access, correction, account closure or deletion, email mirai.labs.health@gmail.com from the address associated with your account or application. You do not need an account to make a privacy request. Guest applicants can use this address to request access to or correction or deletion of their application, or to withdraw it from consideration. We may need to verify your identity and clarify the request before taking action. Requests are handled under applicable law; some records may need to be retained for legal, security or transaction purposes. Do not send identification documents unless we have arranged an appropriate verification method. You may clear local browser storage through your browser settings. You may ask to stop optional marketing communications without affecting necessary order, account or application communications.

8. Age restrictions and external services

The service is intended for eligible purchasers aged 21 and over, not children. Contact us if you believe a child has supplied personal information so we can investigate and take appropriate action. Links to external sites and payment apps lead to services with their own practices. Our providers may process information in the United States and other locations where they operate; this policy does not promise that all processing occurs in one jurisdiction.

9. Updates

This page shows its effective date. Material changes to information practices will be communicated as required, and additional consent will be requested when required. A policy update does not authorize an undisclosed incompatible use of information already collected.
